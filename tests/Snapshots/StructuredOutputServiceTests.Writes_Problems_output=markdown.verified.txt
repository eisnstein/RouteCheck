## `Api.csproj`

| Method | Path | Handler |
|---|---|---|
| GET | `/pets/{id}` | A.ById |
| GET | `/pets/{name}` | A.ByName |

### Problems

- GET /pets/{id} (A.ById) and GET /pets/{name} (A.ByName) match the same requests, so requesting them fails with an AmbiguousMatchException. Use different paths or route constraints, e.g. {id:int}.
